# CONFIRMED DIPLOMACY ESPIONAGE REVOLT

## Status
- Confirmed design lock for `v0.69-0 EASTWAR Strategic Simulation Foundation Roadmap Lock`.
- This document is design-only. No diplomacy, espionage, revolt, UI, save/load, battle, invasion, defense, or AI implementation is included in this task.

## Strategic Role
- Diplomacy, espionage, and revolt are v0.69 foundation systems, not visual panel fillers.
- They should build on public support, loyalty, security, trade, and tech state.
- Revolt risk should not be a single random event disconnected from city conditions.

## System Relationships
- Low public support should represent domestic hardship and instability.
- Low loyalty should represent weak voluntary military cooperation.
- Low security should amplify both public disorder and military unreliability.
- Espionage can later exploit weak security, low public support, or low loyalty.
- Diplomacy can later change trade eligibility, threat pressure, and espionage exposure.

## v0.69 Placement
- `v0.69-5 Revolt Warning Foundation MVP` introduces warning-level foundations before full revolt behavior.
- `v0.69-9 Diplomacy/Espionage Foundation MVP` follows trade and tech data foundations.
