# CONFIRMED CITY TECHTREE DESIGN

## Status
- Confirmed design lock for `v0.69-0 EASTWAR Strategic Simulation Foundation Roadmap Lock`.
- This document is design-only. No city tech tree data, city-building UI, save/load, economy, recruitment, revolt, battle, or defense implementation is included in this task.

## Scope
- The city tech tree represents local development and specialization.
- City tech should be city-scoped and should not collapse into the national tech tree.
- City tech can eventually influence livelihood, public support, security, local recruitment, trade capacity, and city defense readiness.

## Design Principles
- City tech should grow from city identity and strategic role: hub, rear, frontline, trade city, border city, or unstable city.
- City tech should support the public support / loyalty / security split instead of bypassing it.
- City tech data should be added before final UI/UX.

## v0.69 Placement
- `v0.69-7 City Tech Tree Data MVP` follows the national tech data pass.
- UI is deferred until after the strategy logic foundation is stable.
