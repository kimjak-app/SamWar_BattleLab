# General Management, Recruitment & Loyalty Detail Transaction

Status: Future requirement; not implemented by T02.

T02 deliberately has no personal loyalty field, UI, betrayal calculation, prison, captive, or free-agent system. Conquest-aligned generals remain ordinary faction generals, optionally carrying `acquisition_type=conquest_mvp`, `acquired_from_faction_id`, and `acquired_transaction_id` as migration provenance.

The future transaction must provide personal loyalty from 0 to 100; initial loyalty for existing generals; lower initial loyalty for conquest-aligned generals; rewards and appointments; victory/defeat and prolonged non-appointment effects; betrayal, flight, enemy persuasion, and recruitment probability; prison/captive/release/execution and free-agent connections; save/load migration; visible UI; and AI general management.
